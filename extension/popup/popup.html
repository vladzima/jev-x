Privacy

While filtering is enabled, the text of posts on your home timeline and lists is sent to your backend and to TypeSafe (Jev) for scoring. Images, videos, and thread context are not included. DMs, notifications, profiles and search are not read. Scores are cached so repeat posts are not re-scored.

This extension reads page content in your browser. Whether reading X pages this way is acceptable under X's current terms is your call to make — check X's developer/automation policies before relying on it, and use your own account only.
